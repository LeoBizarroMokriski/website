About

I am currently a PhD student at UC Santa Barbara studying mathematics. I graduated from Emory University with a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics and Bachelor of Arts in Philosophy. My research interests include Functional Analysis, Partial Differential Equations, and Mathematical Physics. My past work has focused on fluid dynamics, conformal mappings, and Koopman-von Neumann mechanics.

Beyond mathematics, I'm also interested in philosophy; in particular, I'm fascinated by metaphysics, ontology, and existentialism. Additionally, I enjoy reading, surfing, video games, and playing guitar. I grew up in New Jersey, and I am also half Portuguese. My favorite color is perano, which is the background of my website.
